# About me

I live in Romania with my wife, four kids and two dogs.

# Space credentials

I worked in European Space Operations for 11 years from 2005 to 2016, at [Heavens-Above GmbH][heavens-above], at the [DLR][dlr] campus in [Oberpfaffenhofen][oberpfaffenhofen].

I worked on the following major missions:

[Columbus][columbus] (ISS module): Telemetry display system [satmon][satmon], telemetry archive, control room displays.

[TerraSAR-X][terrasar]/[TanDEM-X][tandem] SAR mission: [Mission planning system][mps], telemetry display system satmon, telemetry archive.

[SAR-Lupe][sarlupe] 1-5: Satmon, proprietary command console GUI

[SATCOMBw][satcombw] 1/2: Satmon

[EUMETSAT][eumetsat] [MetOp A/B][metop]: Satmon

[Eutelsat][eutelsat]: Satmon

# Software credentials

I have been programing since I was 12, starting on an Atari 800. Yes, I am pretty old.

In addition to the proprietary ground segment software above, I worked on several open source projects.

- The [Scala][scala] language
- The [Spire][spire] abstract algebra library
- A distributed event sourcing system for [Actyx][actyx].

Currently I am working on [iroh], a networking library for direct peer to peer connections.

I like both highly abstract fuctional programming and low level programming.
